# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Confirmed by the owner: "a developer who wants to showcase skills to the outside world", for a new role or a collaboration on an indie project. The owner delegated prioritisation.

Inferred (owner delegated): two visitor scenes, both skimming in under a minute.
- **Hiring side**: engineering managers, recruiters and founders weighing M Santhosh Kumar for a senior or lead role. They want to know what he has shipped, at what scale, and how senior he is, and they leave with a résumé or an email.
- **Collaborator side**: developers who found news-fetch, Dev Tools or a package and want to know who built it and whether he would be good to build with. They leave by opening the work or emailing.

## Product Purpose
A personal site for M Santhosh Kumar, a Technical Lead at Digitap.ai (Bengaluru) and data engineer. Success means a visitor quickly forms the view "experienced builder who ships real, used tools" and then emails, downloads the résumé, or opens the work. The owner asked that the site leave a strong impression and not feel bloated or like an endless scroll.

## Positioning
He ships things other developers use: news-fetch (Python article extractor, 230+ GitHub stars, 110+ forks, v1.1.0 in Nov 2026), Dev Tools at devtools.digitap.ai (39+ privacy-first browser utilities built at Digitap.ai), and 11 packages on PyPI and npm. He also leads a team and owns architecture at a fintech company.

## Operating Context
Static site on GitHub Pages at https://santhoshse7en.github.io/cv/, deployed by a GitHub Actions workflow that uploads the repo root. The site is plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with no build step. The résumé PDF is hosted on Google Drive.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must stay a static, no-build site served from the repo root.
- Content that must be preserved: experience timeline (Digitap.ai, Fanlytiks, CSIR-CDRI, 24 Frames Factory), education (JNTU Anantapur B.Tech CSE 2014–2018), awards (SIH 2018 runner-up, Berkeley hackathon 2017 runner-up), publication (IJRTE 2019), packages, GitHub projects, and contact links (email, LinkedIn, GitHub, PyPI, npm).
- The owner delegated prioritisation: older and toy items may be compressed but not deleted.

## Evidence on Hand
- news-fetch: live GitHub star and fork badges, CHANGELOG at ../news-fetch, docs at santhoshse7en.github.io/newsfetch_doc.
- Dev Tools: live site devtools.digitap.ai, with "39+ tools, 8 categories, privacy-first, offline, no signup" taken from the owner's screenshots and the site's meta description.
- Package registries: PyPI and npm profiles.
- Absent: no testimonials, no client logos, no portrait photo, no usage numbers beyond public badges. Do not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles
1. Lead with shipped work, not adjectives. Every claim should point at something a visitor can open.
2. Respect a one-minute skim. The essentials (who, seniority, best work, how to reach him) belong in the first two screens.
3. Recent and used work first. Older and toy projects stay, but compressed.
4. Signal seniority through restraint and precision, not effects.
