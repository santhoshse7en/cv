# Surface: personal site (index.html)

Mode: Experience. Audience: hiring managers and founders skimming for seniority, and developers who found the work and are weighing a collaboration. Actions: email, download the résumé, open the work. Constraints: static GitHub Pages site with no build step; all existing factual content stays, older items compressed. Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The career set as a developer-conference programme: two keynotes, track-coloured sessions and one timetable. It refuses the category default of a dark neon hero plus a stack of equal cards and an endless scroll.

OWN-WORLD: Cool programme-stock paper (#f4f5f1) with black ink (#121212). Four track colours used as full fields, not accents: Python blue #2f5bd3, npm red #c4391f, GitHub green #19774e, Product yellow #f2c230. 1px and 2px ink timetable rules. Archivo, expanded heavy for titles and regular for body, with tabular numerals for dates. No cards, glow, gradients or eyebrows.

STORY: In the first viewport the visitor learns who he is, his seniority, and the two things he shipped that people use, and has email and résumé to hand. The timetable proves range, the career line proves experience, and the close asks for contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top row: event-title name across about two-thirds of the width, with a registration block on the right (open-to line, email, résumé, profiles). Below it, two keynote bands side by side filling the width: news-fetch as a saturated blue field and Dev Tools as a saturated yellow field. Each has a date and version, a large title, a one-line proof (stars, tool count) and working links. The primary action is the email button in the registration block.
